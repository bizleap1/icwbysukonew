# SUKO Atelier Luxury Design & Email Guidelines

This rule enforces the bespoke luxury aesthetic of SUKO Atelier (The Indian Corporate Wear) across all frontend components, transactional emails, invoices, billing, and notification templates.

## Core Brand Palette
- **Canvas / Backgrounds:** `#FAF8F5`, `#F7F4EE`, `#FFFFFF` for pristine cards. (Never use cold blues or generic dark grays).
- **Primary Text & Headings:** `#111113` (Deep Obsidian Charcoal).
- **Muted & Secondary Copy:** `#6E6E75`, `#7A7A85`.
- **Atelier Accent & Badges:** `#C2922E`, `#D4AF37` (Champagne Gold).
- **Borders & Dividers:** Delicate `#EAE6DF`, `#D8D4CC`, `#F3EFE6`.

## Typography & Editorial Style
- **Tracking / Letter-spacing:** Generous tracking on brand marks and badges (e.g. `letter-spacing: 0.22em` or `0.32em` for `S U K O`).
- **Uppercase Eyebrows:** Small uppercase subtitles in champagne gold (e.g. `THE INDIAN CORPORATE WEAR &bull; ATELIER`).
- **Currency:** Indian Rupee formatting with symbol (`₹` e.g. `₹14,999.00`).

## Transactional Emails & Invoices
- **Sender:** Always `SUKO Atelier <noreply@indiancorporatewear.com>`.
- **Reply-To & Concierge:** Always `indiancorporatewearbysuko@gmail.com`.
- **Designated Testing Email:** `meshramshreya042@gmail.com` (Never send tests to `meshramshreya28@gmail.com`).
- **Format:** Always provide dual multipart (`text` plain-text fallback + responsive luxury `html`).
- **Invoice Structure:**
  - Distinctive `S U K O` atelier masthead.
  - Official invoice number `#INV-SUKO-{orderId}` with order date and payment status badge.
  - Delivery destination block.
  - Itemized table with garment title, size badge, unit rate, quantity, and total.
  - Pricing breakdown highlighting Complimentary Atelier Shipping and grand total in a `#FAF6EE` box with gold border `#C2922E`.
  - Atelier Craftsmanship reassurance and tracking / concierge assistance links.
  - Formal company footer with registered domain `indiancorporatewear.com`.
